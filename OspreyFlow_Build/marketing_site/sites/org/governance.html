Governance

How decisions are made

A short account of who decides what in OspreyFlow, and how that can change.

The steward

Solirius Reply created OspreyFlow, employs its project leads and looks after the project: its name, its repository, its releases and its security process. The code is released under the Apache License 2.0, which lets anyone use, change and distribute it.

Who decides what

DecisionWho decides
What the product does next (the roadmap)The Product Lead, after hearing from users, maintainers and the steward.
Architecture and designThe Chief Architect. Each decision is written down as a numbered record with its reasons.
Whether a change is mergedA maintainer of that area. Changes to access control need two.
Who becomes a maintainerThe project leads together.
ReleasesThe project leads, once every check has passed.

Principles the project holds to

  • Decisions are recorded, with their reasons, where everyone can read them.
  • The same checks apply to everyone's changes, the project leads' included.
  • A disagreement is settled by the person who answers for that area, and the decision is written down.
  • The open-source product is the whole product. Solirius Reply sells services around it, not a separate edition.

Changing this

This page describes how the project runs today, with a small founding team. As contributors join, it will change, and changes will be proposed in the open. A governance file and a list of maintainers are not yet in the repository.

Want help to run it? Solirius Reply offers pilots, builds and support: ospreyflow.net.
