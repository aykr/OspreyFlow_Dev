Services

A pilot, a build, and the running of it

The software is open source and free to use. Solirius Reply provides the people who make it work on your data, in your environment, for your analysts.

Talk to us

Three stages

  1. Pilot

    A few weeks, on your own or anonymised data, with two or three analysts and an administrator.

    • Two or three of your sources loaded through pipelines.
    • A case your analysts already know, rebuilt in OspreyFlow.
    • A short report: what was found, what it took and what a full deployment would need.

  2. Build

    A production deployment in your own tenancy.

    • Pipelines for your sources, scheduled and monitored.
    • Your organisation's sign-in, roles and markings.
    • Your look, including the GOV.UK theme for a gov.uk service.
    • Rules, hotlists and screening lists set up with your teams.

  3. Run and expand

    Support once it is live, and help as it grows.

    • Managed support and updates.
    • New sources, teams and units.
    • Dashboards for leadership.
    • Training for analysts, data engineers and administrators.

What you keep

The software

Apache-2.0. If we part ways, everything keeps running and you can take it forward yourselves or with anyone else.

The data

It never leaves your environment. We work inside your boundary, under your controls.

The configuration

Your domain models, pipelines and rules are files you own and can read.

Deployment options

Your cloud account

AWS or Azure, on one server or a Kubernetes cluster, using the cloud's managed services.

Your own data centre

One Linux server or a cluster in your network, with your own certificates.

A shared government platform

A profile for shared Kubernetes platforms, in the current release: installed and checked on a test cluster, not yet on a platform.
