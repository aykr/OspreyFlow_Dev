UK public sector

OspreyFlow for a UK public sector service

OspreyFlow has a GOV.UK theme for services on a gov.uk domain, with government security markings, need-to-know access and a full audit trail. Solirius Reply helps a public sector team take it from a pilot to a live service.

Talk to Solirius ReplyThe UK public sector page on ospreyflow.org

What "designed to meet" means

OspreyFlow is designed to meet the GDS Service Standard. Three independent checks are planned and not yet done: an independent accessibility audit, a penetration test and a data protection impact assessment (DPIA). Only a service assessment panel decides whether a service meets the Standard, and it looks at a service, not at a product alone.

How Solirius Reply helps a public sector team

  • Pilot. A few weeks on your own or anonymised data, under the GOV.UK theme with your service's name.
  • Build. A deployment in your own tenancy, with your organisation's sign-in, roles and markings.
  • Run and expand. Support once it is live, and help as it grows.
