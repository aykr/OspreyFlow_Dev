Four places

Four places, each with one job

OspreyFlow is organised into four places: the Glass for analysis, the Ingest Studio for data, Rules and Monitoring for alerts, and Admin for running the deployment. You see only the places your roles open, and a banner always says where you are and what you are working on.

Built, with parts still coming. The four places, the banner, Admin's sections and roles an administrator can define are built. Where a part is still to come, it is marked Coming; a built section is marked Built. Screenshots follow.

BuiltThe context banner and cross-links

Under the top bar, one line says where you are and what you are working on: in the Glass, You are in the Glass · Case · Boards, with the case and the dataset it reads; elsewhere You are in the Ingest Studio, You are in Rules and Monitoring or You are in Admin.

  • Cross-links keep your place. Someone who holds both the Glass and the Studio can open the case's dataset in the Studio, and come back to the same case and view.
  • A lighter case screen. The view switch shows the graph and the map, with the Matrix and the case's figures one click away.

BuiltAdmin, in sections

Admin is a place of its own, with a side list of sections, each shown only to someone holding its right.

  • People: people and roles, roles and teams, dashboard identities.
  • Data: datasets, connections, proposed types. Each dataset shows its kind, unit, highest allowed marking, its Authors, its linked cases, and whether drawn items are promoted by their author or reviewed by a second person.
  • Running: operations, system, models and maps. Operations lists the promotions waiting for review that you may review, and flags those waiting too long. Model and map keys are write-only: never shown back.
  • Audit: one filtered view over the records that already exist, read with the administrator's own access.
  • Demo packs: the synthetic demonstration packs, addable later.
  • Disabling a person first lists what they answer for and asks who receives each item. Nothing is deleted, and every transfer is audited.
  • ComingPolicies: the grading scheme on screen (a command changes it today), approval rights and uploads; and the rest of what a person owns (views, boards) in the Disable list.

BuiltRoles you can define

  • Built-in roles (Analyst, Data Engineer, Administrator, Reports Viewer and Rule author) stay as they are, and can be copied into a new role.
  • Custom roles are a name and a set of existing rights. No one can give more than they hold.
  • A Super user preset gives Analyst, Data Engineer and Rule author together, and is still never the second person on their own work.
  • Teams and leads. A team has a lead, who may name someone to cover until a date. A dataset's owners may be people, a team, or both, as the deployment allows.
  • ComingTips and who-can. A short tip beside every role and right, and an action you cannot take saying which roles can.

BuiltUnder the GOV.UK theme

With the GOV.UK theme, the switch between the four places sits inside the GOV.UK header and the banner is a GOV.UK notification banner. Admin's sections are a GOV.UK side navigation. Every new screen is checked with automated accessibility scans in the dark, light and GOV.UK themes.
