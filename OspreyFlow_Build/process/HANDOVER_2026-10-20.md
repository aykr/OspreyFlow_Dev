## 3. The golden-set recall gate failed now and then — fixed (48 runs)

`tests/golden/test_golden_set.py` failed on 19 October (`main`) and on 20 October (the `sigma-4` run):
`witness-statement-1/persons_mentioned` recall 0.58 against a 0.60 floor (baseline 0.75, tolerance

Python. The case expects three people, so each run's recall is 0, ⅓, ⅔ or 1, sampled at Ollama's
default temperature, and the gate compares a mean of 12 runs. The tolerance was set from three batch
means (0.69, 0.75, 0.77).

**Measured 20 October** (60 batches of the gate's own statistic, against a throwaway copy of CI's pinned
Ollama and LiteLLM, ARM rather than CI's x86): see §3a. **Proposed, not done — the owner's decision:**
more runs per batch rather than a wider tolerance, shown red against a planted regression before it is
trusted (rule 15), and recorded in the harness docstring and the build plan.

### 3a. The measurement

Sixty batches, each the gate's own statistic (the mean of 12 runs), 20 October 2026:

| | recall | precision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mean of batches | **0.719** | 0.542 |
| spread (standard deviation) | **0.062** | 0.063 |
| lowest, highest | 0.583, 0.861 | 0.403, — |
| batches below the 0.60 floor | **1 of 60** (0.583 — CI's own 0.58) | — |
| batches below 0.65 | 10 of 60 | — |

Read: the floor sits about 1.9 standard deviations under the true mean, so the gate fails on noise
about one run in 35–40 — what CI has shown. The baseline itself (0.75) is above the measured mean
(0.72), being one batch's mean. One run's recall varies by about 0.21; a batch of **48** runs would vary
by about 0.031, putting the 0.60 floor almost four standard deviations below the mean (a false
failure about once in 10,000 runs) while still catching a real drop of 0.15. The golden part of the
conformance job takes about 20 s now, so roughly a minute more per CI run. Measured on this
computer's ARM CPU; CI's x86 may sample a little differently.

**Done (owner, 20 October):** `N_RUNS = 48`, shown red at recall 0.45 with one person planted out and green three times running, and CI green at `6b4943a` (run 39477237459; the step 123.5 s against 39.9 s; build plan, 20 October).
