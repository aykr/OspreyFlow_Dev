# Case lane · L3.3 drawing boards · plan (8 November 2026)

Spec worked from: ADR-143 decisions 1–7 and 13, Amendments 1–3; ADR-144 and its Amendment 2 (a board is a
case view with `drawing` on); ADR-146 decision 4 (watches made by drawing); ADR-105 Amendment 3; spec 01
§2.2c, spec 02 (places, banner), spec 03 §3.1b.

## Files

- `packages/schemas/boards.py` (new) and `Provenance.drawn` in `schemas/domain.py`: one commit at the head.
- `services/api/boards.py` (new): the board service; `services/api/board_store.py` (new): the item
  registry and the drawn-item watches (in-memory and Postgres); routes in `app.py` beside the views'.
- `services/api/case_views.py`: `save(..., drawing=)`; `visible_in` keeps boards out of the views list.
- `apps/workspace/src/boards/**` (new): the Boards place, the board surface, the item, link, picture and
  match panels, GOV.UK forms; `keyboard/boards-harness.*`, `keyboard/boards.spec.ts`; the a11y harness list.
- Outside the map (ratify): the banner's *Boards* (shell), `App.tsx` routing, `a11y/every-harness.spec.ts`.

## Tests first (each red by a plant at its real call site, recorded in `L3.3_RED_RECORD.md`)

1. Rule 3/4: every board route refuses a non-member as a missing case, including a member of another case
   reading the same source; a drawn item is never readable outside its case (plant: the store's case
   filter removed; the item read with the case's layer added to case B's read set).
2. Decision 4: every drawn item carries `asserted_by`, board and case, marking, grading, source note,
   `analyst_drawn` (plant: the grading dropped at the writer).
3. Amendment 2 decision 3: an item above the case's ceiling is refused at the route, in words naming it
   (plant: the ceiling check bypassed).
4. Amendment 2 decision 5 and Amendment 3 decision 1: a drawing with no case makes a drawn dataset, its
   home case and its board; its items are written to the drawn dataset and to no layer.
5. Amendment 2 decision 8: drawing sets one watch owned by the author; withdrawing ends it; the watch list
   is withheld whole from a reader who may not see the item.
6. Rule 6 / decision 6: *Same* writes one `SAME_AS` (1.0) in the layer; both nodes stay, the loaded one
   unchanged (plant: the writer upserting the drawn node under the loaded node's id).
7. Decision 7: an uploaded picture's Document and `DEPICTS` are in the layer, never the source.
8. Amendment 2 decision 4: the restore test, live under a lease (graph backup and restore on a throwaway
   database: the item and its picture come back in the right case's layer and no other).
9. Rule 13: `keyboard/boards.spec.ts` for declare, link form from the selected item, move by arrows,
   inspect, edit, withdraw, match, picture; registry ids as a diff. Axe in dark, light and GOV.UK.
