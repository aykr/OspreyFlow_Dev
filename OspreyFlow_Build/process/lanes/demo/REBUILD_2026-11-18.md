## What changes, and why it is a new deployment

v0.6.0 is installed fresh (the owner's ruling 2, `rf`): `harness/fresh_only.py` refuses an install over a
deployment whose `deployment.env` names an earlier release, before anything changes. osprey-small (in
`/opt/ospreyflow`, the v0.5.2 candidate 574a05c) therefore stays as it is, stopped, as the fall-back; v0.6.0 is
installed into **`/opt/ospreyflow-v0.6.0`**, a folder of its own.

Two facts shape the steps (docs' finding F2, the orchestrator's ruling):

- On Small, `install --name` is refused ("A Small server … is a deployment of its own: install it without
  --name"), and `install.yaml` cannot carry `deployment.name`. So the new folder renders the same Compose project,
  **`osprey-small`**, and would find the old deployment's volumes. They are removed first, after a verified
  backup, as on 12 November (`REPORT.md`), except `osprey-small_ollama-models` and `osprey-small_whisper-models`.
- The old folder keeps its `deploy/generated/small/` and its code, so the way back is a restore there (below).
