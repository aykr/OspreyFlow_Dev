**Where the text comes from:** the "Waiting, on neither machine" box is `Claude outputs/ospreyflow_build_plan_2026-11-17/dashboard/index.html` about lines 492 to 521 (the dashboard's own script), reading `status.js`, which only `OspreyFlow_Build/scripts/slice_line/board.py` writes. A fix to a label is in one of those two files; both are the line-fix session's, not the conductor's.

**What the owner could not tell from it (23 November, 07:30 UTC):** four items sat under one heading, "Waiting, on neither machine (4)", and he asked whether they all meant "done and waiting for the merge queue". They did not. Each waited for something different, and one was wrong.
