## The fault (read it yourself before you write a line)

Removing a dashboard identity in the Admin does not remove what that identity's pulls left in the reporting mart. The route `OspreyFlow_Core/services/api/app.py` (the dashboard-identity DELETE, about lines 18174 to 18203; the scout found it there, the inventory said 18100 to 18106) revokes the identity's case grants and deletes the identity, and **never touches the mart**. Pruning happens only in `OspreyFlow_Core/services/report_sync/sync.py` (about line 162, `mart.prune` on the configured list), and that list is the environment variable `REPORT_SYNC_DASHBOARDS`, which nothing in Core writes except backup, restore and the cloud (`harness/reporting.py` about 143, `harness/restore.py` about 547). `services/report_sync/mart.py` (about 119 to 130) deletes figures only when called, and `record_failure` (about 109 to 117) keeps rows on a refusal. **Result: the removed identity's figure and pull rows stay in the mart, and Admin and Alpha, who read the whole mart in Superset by the path ADR-148 Amendment 21 accepts, still see them.** It is a retention fault, and an access-control question only inside that accepted exception: say in your report whether, on your reading, anything beyond Admin and Alpha can read those rows (the guest role: backlog N18, `pulls` readable through the guest role, is a separate row and **not** in this slice: do not fix it here, and do not make it worse).

## Round 5 (the conductor, 22 November 2026, after four rounds: one design, not one more patch)

**The faults of rounds 2, 3 and 4 are one fault: the order of the removal.** Each fix patched a branch of "the identity is deleted at the provider and the mart then fails" and opened the next (no audit row; a rollback that undid the purge; a retry that needs a Remove button the list no longer shows). **Stop patching; build the removal in this order, so that no step's failure can leave a removed person's rows readable** (the scout's table is `IS5a`'s sibling: `SE6b`'s open cases are the round-3 residue and the round-4 retry button, both this one fault):
1. **Commit the removal stamp first, in its own transaction, before the identity provider is called, and never roll it back together with the purge.** The stamp is what makes the rows unreadable: the readers (Superset's reads, the mart's views) hide every stamped identity **at once**, and the sync's `record_failure` and every write path refuse to write a stamped identity's rows.
2. **Then delete at the identity provider.** If that fails, the identity is still in the list (a Remove button is there), its rows are hidden until the retry completes, and **the failed removal is a refusal in words** (the administrator sees it; nothing is silent). A retry of Remove on a still-listed identity is idempotent.
3. **Then purge the rows.** The purge is repeatable and **the next sync pass finishes it**: a stamped identity absent from the provider's list is purged by the sync, so a purge that fails costs nothing (the rows are already hidden) and **no human step is needed**. A purge failure after the provider's delete is a logged, audited warning, not a 503 that tells a person to retry a button that is gone.
4. **Audit the removal once, whichever step fails after the stamp** (the audit row names what completed).
**Tests, red first, one per step's failure** (rule 12 and 15, at the shapes the route really receives): the stamp commits and the provider fails (identity listed, rows hidden, retry works); the provider succeeds and the purge fails (rows hidden, the next sync purges them, no button needed); the provider raises **`NoSuchDashboard`, the error Keycloak raises** (every route fake so far raised only Entra's `PersonActionRefused`: the open test gap), and one case for each; an audit row in each. **Rule 4:** a reader test that a stamped identity's rows are not returned to Admin or Alpha at any step. **Report: for every step that can fail, what a reader can see afterwards (it must be nothing of a removed identity's).** Do not weaken an earlier round's test.
