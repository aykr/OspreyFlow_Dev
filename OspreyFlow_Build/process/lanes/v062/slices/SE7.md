## What the scout found (read, not run)

`OspreyFlow_Core/services/api/case_views.py` lines 71 to 80: `wire()` sets `chosen` to the stored count of the view's nodes less the merged-added ones, and takes no reader, so it is the unfiltered count. It is returned by the list route (about app.py 4705 to 4708, from `visible_in` at case_views.py 388), the save route
(about app.py 4732), the share route (about app.py 4829) and the open route (case_views.py 599 to 601). **A reader therefore learns how many nodes a shared view holds, including ones above their clearance.** A second leak: case_views.py line 583 computes `missing` as the chosen nodes the reader cannot see, which tells them how many are
hidden (the API text at about app.py 4791 says it "names none"). count there needs a **count-visible method on the graph port** (rule 3: access is applied in the adapter, never after retrieval). The boards code already holds the stricter rule (services/api/boards.py about lines 412 to 415: a missing count only for items lost after placement; anything hidden by a marking is counted nowhere).

## Round 2 (the conductor, 20 November night, on the round-1 verifier's fault; a security fault: it is fixed, never accepted)

The verifier found one fault in `OspreyFlow_Core/services/api/case_views.py` (`missing_for`, about lines 596 to 607): **for the view's creator it returns `len(chosen ids) - len(nodes shown)`, which counts every chosen node the creator cannot read now** (one re-marked above their clearance after the save, one in a dataset the case does not read, and every chosen node if the creator's own clearance is lowered). The owner's confirmed point 2 says the opposite, for anyone: **`missing` counts only items lost after the view was placed (deleted or removed); anything hidden by a marking is counted nowhere** (the boards' rule, `services/api/boards.py` about lines 412 to 422, which counts only items whose `withdrawn_at` is set). The new specification text (`spec/01_DOMAIN_MODEL.md` about lines 749 to 751 and 770) cites "the boards' rule" but leaves out its second half. Your docstring says why you took the difference ("a store cannot tell a node that was deleted from one the reader may not see without an unfiltered lookup"): that is the real problem, and **an existence lookup is not an answer, it is an oracle** (a count that differs for an id that does not exist and an id that exists but is hidden lets a principal probe for hidden nodes; rule 3).
