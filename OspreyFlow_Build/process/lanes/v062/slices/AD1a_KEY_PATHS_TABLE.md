# AD1a: every point in the model key paths where a key or credential exists and a later step can fail or be interrupted

Made on the owner's word of 20 November 2026 (item 1: "list before judging") by a read-only scout of AD1a's branch at its round-3 tip `d85fdbc` (the verifier's checkout), from reading, not running. No key appears in it.
**How round 4 is judged (the owner, item 2):** against this table, not only against the round-3 finding. A row is a **fault** when a later call could send a key to an address it was not given for, or show it back, log it or audit it (columns 5 and 6).
**Any fault row round 4 does not close stops AD1a and comes to the owner with this table. No standing accept, no narrower accept.** The SE5 case (a failed or interrupted address change, then a failed undo, then a later key set) is added as row 28.

Abbreviations: MA = `OspreyFlow_Core/services/api/model_admin.py`; tests under `OspreyFlow_Build/tests/services/api/`: TR = `test_model_admin_routes.py`, TE = `test_model_edit_failure_keeps_key_off_old_address.py`, TC = `test_model_engine_checks.py`.

findings, the key put before the old entries are withdrawn, appears fixed at this tip: withdraw runs before put, MA 1344 to 1346). Rows 15, 16 and 17 are not covered by either finding.
